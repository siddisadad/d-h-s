# 18 Future Roadmap: DCI ERP 2026-2027

## 1.1 Version 1.1 (Stable)
- **Barcode Scanning**: Camera integration for rapid inventory auditing.
- **Cloud Printing**: Direct integration with thermal yard printers.

## 1.2 Version 2.0 (Multi-Entity)
- **Multi-Warehouse Support**: Tracking stock transfers between yards.
- **Offline Mode**: Re-implementation of Drift for true offline-to-online resilience with sync queues.

## 1.3 Version 3.0 (Intelligence)
- **AI Demand Forecasting**: Predicting steel demand based on seasonal construction trends.
- **Automated Procurement**: Auto-generating purchase orders when stock hits "Critical Low".

## 1.4 Expansion
- **Parent/Client App**: A dedicated app for your customers to track their own orders and ledgers.
- **White-Labeling**: Modular architecture allows re-branding for other hardware enterprises.
