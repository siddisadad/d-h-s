# Module: CRM (Customer & Supplier Management)

## 4.1 Purpose
Manages all external business relationships and tracks financial interaction history.

## 4.2 Features
- **Unified Directory**: Searchable list of all Customers and Suppliers.
- **Ledger Entries**: Detailed view of all historical transactions (Sales, Payments, Credits).
- **Communication Bridge**: Direct "One-Tap" WhatsApp and Call integration.
- **Tax Data**: Management of GSTIN and business locations.

## 4.3 Permissions
| Role | Access |
| :--- | :--- |
| Admin | Full CRUD on all contacts. |
| Manager | View and Edit existing contacts. |
| Employee | View Only. |

## 4.4 Future Improvements
- **Credit Scoring**: Automatic risk assessment based on payment delays.
- **Document Vault**: Uploading PAN cards or tax certificates per contact.
