# Module: Business Analytics & Reporting

## 8.1 Purpose
The strategic intelligence layer of the ERP. Turns raw transaction data into actionable growth insights.

## 8.2 Features
- **Performance Matrix**: Comparative trend charts for "Sales vs Purchases".
- **Profit & Loss Dashboard**: Dynamic calculation of Gross Margin and Net Profit.
- **Data Export**: Export any analytics report to professional Excel or PDF formats.
- **Time-Range Filtering**: Switch views between Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, and Yearly data.

## 8.3 Key Metrics
- **Net Margin %**: (Net Profit / Gross Sales) * 100.
- **Burn Rate**: Monthly average of operating expenses.
- **Stock Turn**: Speed of inventory turnover for specific steel grades.

## 8.4 Business Rules
-   Analytics data is read-only.
-   Financial figures are calculated in real-time from the transaction ledger.

## 8.5 Future Improvements
-   **Predictive Stocking**: Suggesting purchase orders based on 12-month demand trends.
-   **GST Summary**: Automated summaries for quarterly tax planning.
-   **Multi-Series Charts**: Comparing branch-wise performance in a single view.
