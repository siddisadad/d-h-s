# 02 Product Vision: DCI ERP

## 2.1 Vision Statement
To empower industrial commercial enterprises with an intelligent, zero-friction interface that turns operational data into strategic growth.

## 2.2 Mission Statement
Our mission is to build the world’s most reliable and responsive industrial ERP, providing a unified digital workspace for the hardware and steel industry that works anywhere, on any device.

## 2.3 Strategic Goals
1.  **Technological Leadership**: Maintain a cutting-edge tech stack (Flutter, Riverpod, Spring Boot) to deliver superior performance.
2.  **User-Centric Design**: Prioritize industrial-grade usability, ensuring that field workers and office staff can use the app with zero training.
3.  **Data Integrity**: Ensure 100% data consistency across cloud and local in-memory caches.

## 2.4 Core Values
*   **Reliability**: Enterprise systems must never fail during peak business hours.
*   **Precision**: In the steel industry, every kilogram and every rupee matters.
*   **Speed**: Information must flow at the speed of business, with zero UI lag.
*   **Security**: Protecting commercial data is our highest priority.

## 2.5 Long-Term Roadmap
*   **Q4 2026**: Multi-warehouse / Multi-branch support.
*   **Q1 2027**: AI-powered dynamic pricing based on market steel rate fluctuations.
*   **Q2 2027**: Offline-First data synchronization (Re-introduction of Drift with Conflict Resolution).
*   **Q3 2027**: White-label marketplace for steel manufacturers.

## 2.6 Competitive Advantage
Compared to generic ERPs (SAP, Oracle), DCI ERP offers:
*   **Vertical Specialization**: Specific features for steel grades, HSN codes, and industrial unit conversions.
*   **Mobile-First Performance**: Designed specifically for low-latency responsiveness in hardware yards.
*   **Deployment Flexibility**: Easily scalable cloud-native architecture.
